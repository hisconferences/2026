Conference Venue

New Yantai Hotel

  • Conference venue: New Yantai Hotel
  • Address: No. 18 Wudong Road, Meilan District, Haikou, Hainan, 570208, China
Special Accommodation Rate

A special accommodation rate of RMB 360 per room per night, including breakfast for two guests, has been arranged for conference participants.

This preferential rate is available exclusively through the conference organisers and is not available through online booking platforms.

Advance Reservation Required

As the hotel will be hosting other events during the conference period, room availability is limited. Reservations at the special conference rate will be arranged on a first-come, first-served basis, subject to availability.

Participants wishing to take advantage of this rate are strongly encouraged to make their accommodation arrangements as early as possible.

How to Arrange Your Accommodation

Please contact Mr Hong Wei (洪伟) in advance at wei.hong2@live.vu.edu.au and provide the following information:

  • Your name
  • Check-in and check-out dates
  • Number of guests and rooms required

Mr Hong will coordinate the reservations directly with the hotel's designated manager to secure the special conference rate.

Important: To access the discounted rate, please arrange your accommodation through Mr Hong rather than booking directly through online platforms.

We look forward to welcoming you to Haikou!
